Matchup Breakdowns

Position-by-position projections, scored on our system, using live weekly consensus stat projections rather than preseason baselines.

Scoring uses the verified Roster Wars formulas (PPR + rushing for RB/WR, 1.5/rec TE premium, position-weighted IDP, distance-scaled kicker). Point values are converted from live Week 1 stat projections, not the preseason pts_proj baseline. The amber dot marks the closest projected matchup in each conference. Use the arrows to step through positions — the tally below tracks the running score through however far you've gone. Actual results reflect final locked rosters, which occasionally differ from the lineups used for the prediction if a manager made a late swap.
